# Operations notes, October 2026

## Outlet closures

The Indiranagar outlet was closed from 12 to 20 October for kitchen renovation. Staff were moved to the Koramangala outlet during that week.

## Opening hours

From 1 October every outlet opens at 8 in the morning and closes at 23 at night on weekdays. On Saturday and Sunday closing time is midnight.

## Suppliers

The dairy supplier changed in October. Deliveries of milk now arrive on Monday, Wednesday and Friday before 7 in the morning. A late delivery must be reported to the area manager the same day.

## Equipment

The espresso machine at the Whitefield outlet was replaced on 5 October. The old machine failed twice in September, which is why coffee sales there were lower that month.
