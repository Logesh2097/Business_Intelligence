# Refund and exchange policy

## Refunds

A customer may ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase. The original receipt or the order number is required. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Food and drinks

Prepared food and drinks cannot be refunded once served, unless the order was wrong or the quality was poor. In that case the outlet manager replaces the item or refunds it on the spot.

## Packaged goods

Packaged coffee beans and merchandise can be exchanged within 30 days if the seal is unbroken. Opened packages are exchanged only when the product is faulty.

## Gift cards

Gift cards cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash. A lost gift card is replaced only when the customer shows the purchase receipt.
