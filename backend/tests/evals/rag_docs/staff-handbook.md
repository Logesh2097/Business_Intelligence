# Staff handbook

## Leave

Every full-time employee has 24 days of paid annual leave. Leave must be requested from the outlet manager at least two weeks ahead. Unused leave of up to 6 days is carried over to the next year.

## Uniform

Staff wear the black apron and the name badge at all times on shift. The company provides two aprons a year; a replacement costs 350 rupees.

## Cash handling

The till is counted at the start and at the end of every shift by two people. A difference of more than 200 rupees is written in the shift log and reported to the area manager.

## Loyalty programme

Members of the loyalty programme earn one point for every 100 rupees spent. Ten points give a free regular coffee. Points expire after 12 months without a purchase.
